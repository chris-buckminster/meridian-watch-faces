Meridian Watch Faces Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Chris Buckminster

This policy covers the Meridian Blue, Meridian Green and Meridian Night watch faces for Wear OS, published on Google Play by Chris Buckminster.

What these watch faces collect

Nothing. The watch faces do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data.

  • They contain no code: they are display-only designs drawn by your watch's own system.
  • They request no permissions and have no internet access.
  • They include no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party services.
  • They read the time and date from your watch only to show them on screen. That information never leaves the watch.

Purchases through Google Play

Buying a watch face is handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment and account details under the Google Privacy Policy. The developer receives only the aggregate sales and statistics Google Play provides to all developers, not your payment details.

Children

The watch faces are not directed at children under 13 and collect no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
